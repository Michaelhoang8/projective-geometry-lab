03 / NUMERICAL GEOMETRY

Normalization, rank and points at infinity

4 pairs → 8 × 9 system

A minimal DLT fit needs the ninth right-singular vector. The implementation requests the full V basis for an 8 × 9 matrix; a reduced basis would omit its null vector.

Both coordinate sets are centered and scaled to mean distance √2 before estimation. Matrices use unit Frobenius norm with a fixed sign convention, so a valid transform with h₃₃ = 0 remains representable.

RANSAC's adaptive limit uses the observed consensus to estimate the probability of drawing four inliers. The 0.995 confidence setting is a conditional sampling assumption. It does not guarantee a correct model, particularly with structured outliers, degenerate samples or a misspecified threshold.
